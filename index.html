01 / My story

Moving to New York changed the way I think about opportunity.

I was born in Moscow and moved to New York City. Starting over in a different country taught me to adapt quickly, ask questions, and become comfortable learning things I had never encountered before.

Those qualities eventually drew me toward finance. I became fascinated by how a decision that looks like a number on a spreadsheet can represent a much bigger story: a company taking a risk, an investor making a bet, or a market reacting to something happening halfway around the world.

At Baruch, I chose Finance because I wanted a framework for understanding those decisions. I’m still early in that journey, which is exactly what I like about it—I have a lot left to learn.
